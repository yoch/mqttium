# 0064 — Direct effect dispatch, compact session/delivery state, ownership-moving volatile persistence, and fail-closed WAL compaction (Lot 8)

Status: **Accepted with amendments** (27 November 2026, by the repository owner after the validation of `9dfd568`, which corrected the text where the code contradicted it, report 0043; proposed on 24 November 2026). Applied to `mqttiumd-contract`, `mqttiumd-core`, `mqttiumd-store-volatile`, `mqttiumd-store-sqlite`, `mqttiumd-lab`, and `mqttiumd`. [Report 0039](../reports/0039-lot8-arm64-qos1-throughput-and-idle-ram.md) holds the ARM64 empirical A/B measurements and full verification ledger. Its figures are not part of the decision: a single run, on code that later commits changed (report 0043).

No wire format, canonical recipe encoding, SQLite schema, or `.mlog` record encoding changes. `crates/mqttiumd-contract/src/capacity.rs` is untouched, keeping `proofs/credits/BODY.sha256` identical, and the contract digest reported by `mqttiumd-lab validate` remains unchanged (`sha256:21e96cc81c1f48d59841d8424fee2290102c6782b025222f2d6d2fe72ccf9bb5`).

## Context

Following Lot 7 (record [0057](0057-flatmap-direct-volatile-and-zero-alloc-framing.md), report [0030](0030-lot7-arm64-performance-and-durability.md)), ARM64 `perf` profiling and idle-memory inspection of `mqttiumd` on `290db2be` identified two primary remaining efficiency gaps against Mosquitto, plus one compaction durability edge case in `LogStore`:

1. **Per-message CPU overhead on QoS 1 hot paths (`~54.4k/s` on 32 pairs, `~62.5k/s` on 4 pairs in `volatile` mode)**:
   - **Plan/Change copying and `Outbox` round-trip (`~7.5 %` combined)**: Every `Owner::step` returned a large `Plan` struct by value (~1.2 KiB `memcpy`), and steady-state single-effect transitions still pushed into `Outbox::queue`, allocated a reservation entry, popped from `Outbox`, and re-dispatched into `EffectSink` even when `Outbox` was empty.
   - **Fragmented per-session delivery maps and packet-ID allocation**: `pubsub::State` maintained five separate `FlatMap`s keyed by `SessionId` / `(SessionId, ConnectionGeneration)` (`deliveries_by_session`, `sent_on_gen`, `busy_by_session`, `inline_pending_by_session`, `queued_by_session`) plus a redundant `active_by_gen` map, and `free_packet_id` probed linearly on every QoS 1 admission. `PUBACK` performed redundant lookups across `puback_target` and `outgoing_ack_slot`, and `rearm_ready_work` scanned sessions on steps that could not unblock delivery work.
   - **Per-publish heap allocations and atomic refcount churn**: `AdmitPublication.targets` allocated a `BoundedVec<Target>` on the heap even for the 1:1 publish/subscribe common case; `InlineClaimPlan` cloned `topic`, `payload`, and `properties`; `DirectWriter` borrowed `&Command` and cloned `Arc` handles into `VolatileStore`; `codec` allocated a fresh `Arc<[u8]>` per incoming PUBLISH topic; and `VolatileStore::collect_receipts` dereferenced `Arc<Recipe>` to read `canonical_len`.
   - **Cold/unneeded bookkeeping on hot paths**: `Owner::finished_by_op` maintained a hash table even when `retained_operations <= 256`; `must_wait` scanned active slots even when `unanswered_slots <= 1`; `FlatMap` used a multi-instruction SplitMix64 mixer on ARM64; `Direct` volatile mode dispatched empty `expire_messages_at` sweeps when zero expiring publications existed; and `VolatileStore::identity_was_used` / `exec_publication_with_claim` probed multiple maps even when the new sequence strictly exceeded the active scope high-water mark.
2. **Idle memory footprint per connection (`9,728 B/conn` Plain TCP at 768 conns, `20,931 B/conn` mTLS at 400 conns)**:
   - Per-connection `ToCoreState` and `OutgoingState` retained initial `VecDeque` backing buffers allocated during the CONNECT/CONNACK handshake (`~1.1 KiB/conn`), while `Outbox` pre-reserved a large reservation table (`max_reservations`), and sparse `FlatMap` tables stored `Peer`, `VSession`, `CachedBounds`, and cold `pubsub::Connection` / `Session` fields inline.
3. **`LogStore::compact()` fail-open window after failed WAL replacement**:
   - After `save_state_atomic()` updated the SQLite checkpoint, if `write_fresh_wal_atomic()` failed to replace the `.mlog` file, subsequent calls to `flush_prepared_frame()` could still append to and confirm writes against the stale `.mlog` whose epoch/prefix no longer matched the checkpoint, risking silent loss on restart.

## Decision

### 1. Direct synchronous effect dispatch and in-place transition drafting (`mqttiumd-contract`, `mqttiumd-core`, `mqttiumd`)
- **`Outbox::check_reserve` and `Outbox::dispatch_immediate` (`crates/mqttiumd-contract/src/effects.rs`)**: When `Outbox` is empty and has capacity, `Owner::step_and_dispatch` dispatches transition effects directly into the caller's `EffectSink` with the exact same `EffectId` and FIFO ordering; any `NotSubmitted` refusal falls back cleanly into `Outbox` without dropping or re-identifying an effect (preserving ADR 0006 and the no-dropped-effect invariant).
- **In-place `Draft` in `Owner::step_with_sink` (`crates/mqttiumd-core/src/owner.rs`)**: Production transitions compute and apply against an in-place `Draft` instead of returning a large `Plan` by value, while `Plan`/`PlanStamp` unit tests remain intact under `#[cfg(test)] mod tests`. Cold `MessageExpiry` on `Effect::EmitPublish` and `Effect::EmitQos0` is boxed (`Option<Box<MessageExpiry>>`), shrinking `Effect` and `OwnedEffect`.
- **Single-pass runtime dispatch (`crates/mqttiumd/src/runtime.rs`)**: `Runtime::drain_inputs` routes `Input` through `Owner::step_and_dispatch`, skips `Sink` setup on proven zero-effect steps (`FrameResult` with `!has_held_answers()`, `DeliveryFinished`), uses `Owner::last_observed_puback_ignored()` to avoid duplicate `puback_target` lookups, and gates `rearm_ready_work` via `Owner::last_step_may_unblock_work()` and `Owner::last_inline_ready_target()`.

### 2. Consolidated core tables and O(1) fast paths (`mqttiumd-contract`, `mqttiumd-core`)
- **`TargetVec` (`crates/mqttiumd-contract/src/command.rs`)**: Replaces `BoundedVec<Target>` in `AdmitPublication.targets` with an inline 0/1-element enum (`Empty | One(Target) | Many(BoundedVec<Target>)`), eliminating heap allocation for 1:1 publications while preserving exact canonical encoding and bounds for fan-out.
- **Consolidated `SessionDeliveries` and `used_pi_mask` (`crates/mqttiumd-core/src/owner/pubsub.rs`)**: Merges five per-session delivery maps into a single warm `FlatMap<SessionId, SessionDeliveries>` with an O(1) 64-bit `used_pi_mask` bitmask for `free_packet_id`, deletes redundant `active_by_gen`, fuses `outgoing_ack_slot` to a single lookup, and adds O(1) `outgoing_binding_is_live` and `session_provably_idle_work` fast paths.
- **Dense `BoundedSlab`, `unanswered_slots`, and optional `finished_by_op` (`crates/mqttiumd-core/src/{slab.rs,owner.rs}`)**: Replaces swap-remove slot/delivery vectors with index-stable `BoundedSlab` + `SmallIndexSet`; tracks `unanswered_slots: usize` for O(1) `must_wait`/`preceded` short-circuits; omits `finished_by_op` hash indexing when `retained_operations <= 256`; and moves `topic`/`payload`/`properties` directly out of `Input::Packet` on inline claims.
- **Deterministic ARM64 Fibonacci mixer (`crates/mqttiumd-contract/src/flat_map.rs`)**: Replaces SplitMix64 in `FlatKey::flat_hash` with a deterministic 2-instruction Fibonacci multiply-shift (`x.wrapping_mul(0x9e37_79b9_7f4a_7c15); h ^ (h >> 32)`), preserving zero ambient randomness and full pure-crate boundary compliance.

### 3. Ownership-moving `VolatileStore`, high-water-mark freshness, and `TopicCache` (`mqttiumd-store-volatile`, `mqttiumd`)
- **`VolatileStore::apply_owned` and `DirectWriter` (`crates/mqttiumd-store-volatile/src/lib.rs`, `crates/mqttiumd/src/writer.rs`)**: Moves `Command` by value into `VolatileStore` on the `Direct` path, avoiding `Arc::clone`/`Arc::drop` of `FrozenRecipe` and payload bytes, caching `canonical_len: u32` in `VOperation`, tracking `hot_scope` / `hot_max_pub_seq` / `hot_max_payload_seq` monotonic high-water marks for O(1) freshness checks, tracking `expiring_publications` to skip empty `expire_messages_at` sweeps, and fusing `discard_completed_publication_known`.
- **Direct-mapped `TopicCache` (`crates/mqttiumd/src/codec.rs`)**: A 32-slot direct-mapped cache in `Parser` reuses `Arc<[u8]>` topic allocations for repeated PUBLISH topics without heap allocation per frame.
- **Contention-free reactor `Link::consumed` (`crates/mqttiumd/src/net/reactor.rs`)**: Guards the atomic swap in `Link::consumed` with a load check to eliminate unnecessary ARM64 `swp1_acq_rel` cache-line dirtying when idle.

### 4. Compact idle memory per connection (`mqttiumd`, `mqttiumd-store-volatile`, `mqttiumd-core`)
- **Handshake-phase queue reclamation (`crates/mqttiumd/src/net.rs`)**: Tracks saturating packet/receipt counts (`incoming_seen`, `written_seen`, `sent_seen`); while `< 3` (CONNECT/CONNACK handshake and idle phase), emptying a queue releases its `VecDeque` backing buffer immediately (`~1.1 KiB/conn` saved) while keeping steady-state active connections zero-reallocation. Boxes cold `wire_progress: Option<Box<VecDeque<WireProgress>>>`.
- **Dense `PeerTable` and `SessionTable` (`crates/mqttiumd/src/runtime.rs`, `crates/mqttiumd-store-volatile/src/tables.rs`, `crates/mqttiumd-store-volatile/src/witness.rs`)**: Stores `Peer` and prefix-stripped 56-byte `VSession` records in dense free-listed vectors indexed by compact `u32` slots, compacts `CachedBounds` from 104 B to 32 B, moves cold `pubsub::Connection` / `Session` fields out of line, and caps `Outbox` `max_reservations = 64` (saving ~1.5 MiB base RSS).

### 5. Fail-closed `LogStore::compact()` on WAL replacement failure (`mqttiumd-store-volatile`)
- **`wal_desynced` guard (`crates/mqttiumd-store-volatile/src/log.rs`)**: `LogStore::compact()` sets `self.wal_desynced = true` immediately after `save_state_atomic()` commits the new SQLite checkpoint and clears it only after `write_fresh_wal_atomic()` succeeds. While `self.wal_desynced` is set, `flush_prepared_frame()` refuses every new durable write fail-closed (`StoreError::Io`), preventing acknowledgement of frames that would not survive restart. Covered by OS-level regression test `failed_wal_replacement_suspends_durable_writes_until_repaired` in `crates/mqttiumd-store-volatile/tests/log_conformance.rs`.
- **Amended on 26 November 2026** (record 0055, validation of the lot 10 head): the flag is now `rotation_due`, which records the checkpoint whose log is due, and each write first tries the replacement again. The guard is unchanged: nothing is appended to the stale log, and a write that cannot replace it answers `PendingOrUnknown`. What changed is that the store writes again as soon as the cause is gone; with the flag alone it stayed unable to write until a forced checkpoint (`log_resumes_a_rotation_that_failed_at_the_next_write`).

## Consequences

- **ARM64 throughput & latency (report [0039](../reports/0039-lot8-arm64-qos1-throughput-and-idle-ram.md))**:
  - Plain TCP `volatile-ram` QoS 1 reaches **129,687/s** on 4 pairs (+107.5 % vs Lot 7) and **98,378/s** on 32 pairs (`60,000/s` offered completes at `0.04 / 0.49 ms` vs Lot 7 `54,446/s` at `3.19 / 5.37 ms`).
  - TLS 1.3 mTLS 32p QoS 1 at 100k/s reaches **81,566/s (`2.51 / 3.86 ms`)** vs Mosquitto 2.1.2 **43,168/s (`51.06 / 53.44 ms`)** (1.89× throughput, 20× lower p50); mTLS 32p QoS 0 at 120k/s delivers **119,985/s with 0.0 % loss (`0.29 / 1.59 ms`)** vs Mosquitto 2.1.2's 22.0 % loss (`672.74 / 1229.88 ms`).
  - Plain TCP `log-tmpfs` 32p QoS 1 reaches **59,992/s at 60k/s (`0.16 / 1.93 ms`)** and **75,653/s** at 150k/s offered (4p reaches **92,406/s**).
- **Idle memory footprint**:
  - Plain TCP 768 connections drops from `9,728 B/conn` (Lot 7) to **5,269 B/conn** RSS (**11.0 MiB** total vs Mosquitto 2.1.2 **14.8 MiB** total; 512→768 marginal slope **4,352 B/conn**).
  - TLS 1.3 mTLS 400 connections drops from `20,931 B/conn` (Lot 7) to **14,746 B/conn** RSS (**14.0 MiB** total vs Mosquitto 2.1.2 **43,950 B/conn** / **30.5 MiB** total — 3.0× lower per connection).
- **Safety, purity, and durability**: `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, `#![no_std]` + `alloc` in `mqttiumd-contract`/`mqttiumd-core`, `mqttiumd-lab --test boundary` (17/17), `proofs/credits/BODY.sha256`, and all 1,380 default workspace tests, 334 `kill-points` tests, 288 `test-hooks` tests, 274 oracle unit tests, 13 TLA+ model consistency checks, and 0 open conformance items pass.

## Not chosen

- **Moving MQTT packet decoding to I/O worker threads**: Independent measurement showed a throughput regression on small (256 B) messages due to cross-thread allocation and handoff overhead; core-side decoding with a 32-slot `TopicCache` keeps small-message latency minimal.
- **Modifying `BoundedQueue::pop_front` in `mqttiumd-contract/src/capacity.rs`**: Kept untouched to avoid invalidating the Kani credits proof body digest (`proofs/credits/BODY.sha256`).
- **Multi-target batched inline claims / sharded delivery workers**: Deferred to a future lot focused on fan-out scaling (`1:N` subscriptions), keeping Lot 8 self-contained with zero protocol or command-schema expansion.
