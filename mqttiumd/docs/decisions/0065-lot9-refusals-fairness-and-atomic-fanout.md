# 0065 — Spurious-refusal elimination, sub-table encapsulation, atomic 1:N multi-target claims, and multi-connection fairness (Lot 9)

Status: **Proposed** (25 November 2026). Applied to `mqttiumd-contract`, `mqttiumd-core`, `mqttiumd-store-sqlite`, `mqttiumd-store-volatile`, `mqttiumd-lab`, and `mqttiumd`. [Report 0040](../reports/0040-lot9-arm64-fairness-fanout-and-sharding.md) holds the ARM64 empirical A/B measurements, adversarial matrix results, multi-core `perf` sharding study, and full verification ledger.

This record introduces one additive canonical recipe variant (`Recipe::MultiClaimedPublication`, recipe kind `25`, wrappable inside `Recipe::AnnotatedPublication` kind `23`). Existing recipe kinds `0..=24`, wire formats, SQLite schema 14/15, and `.mlog` framing are unchanged. `crates/mqttiumd-contract/src/capacity.rs` is untouched (`proofs/credits/BODY.sha256` identical: `71378725…`), and the contract digest reported by `mqttiumd-lab validate --contracts contracts/v1` remains unchanged (`sha256:21e96cc81c1f48d59841d8424fee2290102c6782b025222f2d6d2fe72ccf9bb5`).

## Context

Following Lot 8 (record [0064](0064-lot8-direct-dispatch-compact-state-and-wal-safety.md), report [0039](../reports/0039-lot8-arm64-qos1-throughput-and-idle-ram.md)), adversarial load-matrix testing (fan-out `1:4`/`1:16`, fan-in `32:1`, slow-subscriber isolation, session churn under QoS 1, and mixed 256 B + 64 KiB payloads) and structural auditing of `mqttiumd` uncovered five correctness, fairness, and scaling gaps:

1. **Spurious QoS 1 `0x83` refusals under concurrent session churn**:
   - Closing or clean-starting an unrelated session on the reserved control/lifecycle lane could overtake an in-flight `AdmitPublication` / `ClaimedPublication` on the ordinary writer lane, advancing `route_revision` in the store before the queued admission executed and causing `RejectReason::PreconditionFailed` (`PUBACK 0x83`) for a healthy publisher whose targets had not changed (`551` refusals in a 2-second `churn-q1` run in `log` mode).
2. **100 ms full-session `queue_work` rescan, empty `expire_messages` sweeps, and `LoadWork` write-before-read inversion**:
   - `discover_work_at` rescanned all sessions every 100 ms even when work notifications were intact, while `inline_claim_enabled == false` paths never recorded `rescan_needed` on unclaimed admissions.
   - Threaded `Writer` unconditionally executed empty 100 ms `MessageExpiry` sweeps on the control lane even with zero expiring publications, and `VolatileStore::expire_messages` sorted the entire expiring set rather than scanning up to `limit`.
   - In `Writer`, `Effect::LoadWork` was pushed into `deferred_reads` even when the write batch `members` was empty, allowing subsequent `Persist` commands and an `fdatasync` group commit to jump ahead of an initial read and trap new fan-in subscribers outside the inline-claim fast path.
3. **Silent receipt shadowing in `OriginOpTable` across `MAX_GAP_PAD`**:
   - When a control-lane operation arrived more than `MAX_GAP_PAD` (`64`) sequences ahead of the ring buffer in `mqttiumd-store-volatile/src/tables.rs`, it spilled into `cold`. When subsequent ring insertions padded `None` slots across that sequence, `OriginOpTable::get` and `remove` returned `None` early without checking `cold`, causing `collect_receipts` to fail with `ReceiptCollectionRefusal::Gap` and terminating the broker with `CoreFault::ImpossibleInput`.
4. **Unencapsulated core secondary indices and unbatched `1:N` fan-out**:
   - `pubsub::State` held raw `connections` and `deliveries` collections alongside their secondary index maps, making index drift possible without a structural boundary.
   - `Recipe::ClaimedPublication` (kind `24`) only supported a single inline claim (`targets.len() == 1` or single claimed target). On a `1:4` or `1:16` QoS 1 fan-out, every publication paid `1` `AdmitPublication` + `N` `LoadWork`/`ClaimDelivery` + `N` `FinishDelivery` store operations (`2N + 1` decisions instead of `N + 1`).
5. **Single-peer ingress starvation and transient `Receive Maximum` burst demotion**:
   - Under `32:1` fan-in or mixed-rate traffic, high-rate publishers could starve subscriber `PUBACK` ingestion in the shared `inputs` queue, and a single transient burst hitting `active(gen) == receive_maximum` permanently knocked a caught-up subscriber out of inline claims (`caught_up = false`).

## Decision

### 1. Zero-refusal route-mutation serialization (`mqttiumd-core`, `mqttiumd`)
- **`Owner::dispatch` route gate (`crates/mqttiumd-core/src/owner.rs`)**: Track `pending_admissions: usize` in `Owner`. Before dispatching a lifecycle command whose action can remove a session or advance `route_revision` (`Close`, `Expire`, `EndWill { expiry: OnDisconnect }`, or `Open` / `OpenAt` / `OpenWillAt` with `expected: Some(_)` and `clean_start: true`), wait while `has_pending_route_admissions()` is true. Non-destructive resume/takeover actions (`clean_start: false`, `Detach`, `EndWill` with retained session) remain ungated on the reserved lifecycle lane so takeover liveness behind a full ordinary lane (ADR 0029 / ADR 0046) is preserved.
- **`Sink::try_submit` inflight route barrier (`crates/mqttiumd/src/runtime.rs`)**: Track `inflight_route_ops: ScopedFlatMap<EffectId, ()>` for route-dependent recipes already submitted to the writer lane (`AdmitPublication`, `ClaimedPublication`, `MultiClaimedPublication`, `Retained*`, `ExpiringPublication`, `UpdateSubscriptions`, `*ReplaySubscriptions`). Route-mutating lifecycle and will commands return `SubmitBlock::QueueFull` until `inflight_route_ops` drains, preventing the control lane from overtaking queued route-dependent writes in the writer thread.

### 2. Lossless work wake-ups, `has_expiring` mirror, and immediate head-of-queue `LoadWork` (`mqttiumd-core`, `mqttiumd`, `mqttiumd-store-volatile`)
- **Eliminate 100 ms full-session `queue_work` rescan (`runtime.rs`, `owner.rs`, `owner/pubsub.rs`, `owner/will.rs`)**: Always mark unclaimed targets dirty (`rescan_needed = true`) on durable publication admission, retained completion, subscription change, and will admission regardless of `inline_claim_enabled`; retain overflow work notifications in bounded `pending_work` and drain them in `drain_inputs` and `discover_work_at`. Keep the 100 ms full-session fallback strictly behind the `test-hooks` `drop-work-notifications` fault injector.
- **`Writer::has_expiring` atomic mirror (`writer.rs`, `mqttiumd-store-volatile/src/lib.rs`)**: Mirror `StoreBackend::has_expiring_publications()` into an `Arc<AtomicBool>` so threaded `LogStore` and `SqliteStore` skip empty 100 ms `MessageExpiry` sweeps without waking the writer thread. Replace full sorting in `VolatileStore::expire_messages` with an O(N) top-`limit` scan.
- **Immediate head-of-queue `LoadWork` (`writer.rs`)**: Only defer `Effect::LoadWork` into `deferred_reads` when `!members.is_empty()`. When `LoadWork` arrives at the head of the writer queue, execute it immediately without batching subsequent writes and an `fdatasync` ahead of it.

### 3. `OriginOpTable` gap-promotion fix (`mqttiumd-store-volatile`)
- **`OriginOpTable::{insert, get, remove}` (`crates/mqttiumd-store-volatile/src/tables.rs`)**: When `insert` pads gaps across sequences present in `cold`, promote those entries into the ring rather than shadowing them with `None`; in `get` and `remove`, check `cold` whenever a ring slot is `None`. Covered by direct regression and 10,000-step randomized differential tests against `BTreeMap`.

### 4. Sub-table encapsulation with step-level index invariant checks (`mqttiumd-core`, `mqttiumd`)
- **`Connections` and `Deliveries` (`crates/mqttiumd-core/src/owner/{connections.rs,deliveries.rs}`)**: Extract online connection settings/cursors/read-slots into `Connections` and outgoing deliveries plus all 11 secondary indices into `Deliveries`, enforcing index maintenance through narrow mutators (`push`, `remove`, `mutate`, `rewrite`, `complete_page`, `begin_scan`).
- **`net::reactor::Shared` (`crates/mqttiumd/src/net/reactor.rs`)**: Encapsulate reactor queue/sleep/hold/leave/stop protocol methods on `Shared`.
- **`Owner::step_checked` (`crates/mqttiumd-core/src/owner/pubsub/tests.rs`)**: In the core's tests, `State::assert_indices_kept` verifies after every step that every incremental secondary index of `Connections` and `Deliveries` matches a fresh rebuild.

### 5. Atomic `1:N` `Recipe::MultiClaimedPublication`, bounded pre-claim headroom, and multi-connection fairness (`mqttiumd-contract`, `mqttiumd-core`, `mqttiumd-store-*`, `mqttiumd-lab`, `mqttiumd`)
- **`Recipe::MultiClaimedPublication` (recipe kind `25`, `crates/mqttiumd-contract/src/command.rs`)**: Carries an `AdmitPublication` plus `claims: BoundedVec<InlineClaim>` (`claims.len() >= 2`, strictly ascending `target_index < admission.targets.len()`, distinct `target.session`s, `!admission.retain`, `effective_qos != AtMostOnce`, wrappable by `AnnotatedPublication` kind `23`). Stack fast-path canonical encoding widened to `STACK_CAP = 768`.
- **Unified multi-claim store execution (`mqttiumd-store-sqlite`, `mqttiumd-store-volatile`, `mqttiumd-lab`)**: All three stores validate and execute `inline_claims: &[InlineClaim]` atomically within the admission transaction, charging `exchange_slots += inline_claims.len()` and `receipt_slots = 1`. Slow or offline targets remain unclaimed in the same publication so fast online targets progress immediately without waiting on slow subscribers.
- **Core multi-target inline claim plumbing (`owner.rs`, `owner/pubsub.rs`, `owner/deliveries.rs`)**: `Deliveries::by_claim_op` maps `OperationId -> SmallIndexSet`; `Owner::plan_admit` emits `ClaimedPublication` for 1 online claim and `MultiClaimedPublication` for `>= 2`, recording only unclaimed sessions in `dirty_targets` and extra inline-ready sessions in `last_inline_ready_extra` without widening `MAX_TRANSITION_SLOTS = 2`.
- **Bounded inline pre-claim headroom (`owner/pubsub.rs`, `owner/deliveries.rs`)**: Allow caught-up subscribers to inline-claim up to `preclaim_cap = rm + rm.min(256)` total deliveries while strictly enforcing `Receive Maximum` on the wire: `apply_inline_claim_insert` assigns `d.window = Some(generation)` only when `active(generation) < rm` and no older unsent delivery lacks a window reservation (preventing window-slot inversion deadlock); `plan_delivery_emit` and `Change::Emit` enforce `active(generation) < rm` at emission time; `SessionDeliveries::used_pi_mask` widens to `[u64; 8]` (512 PIs) for O(1) allocation across the full burst window.
- **Prioritized ACK lane and fair multi-connection ingress (`runtime.rs`)**: Route `ObservedPuback` and `ObservedQos2Ack` into a dedicated `ack_inputs` queue drained ahead of new publishes; cap non-ACK ingress per peer per turn (`max_non_ack_per_peer = (512 / active_peer_count).clamp(16, 32)` for `> 16` active peers) while bounding non-ACK `drain_incoming` chunks to `max_non_ack - non_ack_drained` (eliminating `push_front_incoming` re-queuing and double mutex locks on publishers) and resuming round-robin from `starved_offset` whenever ordinary room fills.

## Consequences

- **Zero spurious refusals under churn & slow-subscriber isolation (report [0040](../reports/0040-lot9-arm64-fairness-fanout-and-sharding.md))**:
  - `churn-q1` (`log`): `551` spurious `0x83` refusals -> **`0` refusals** (`30,000 deliv/s`, `3,496` churn cycles vs Mosquitto `2,458`, `p50 = 0.08 ms`).
  - `slow-sub-q1` (`log`): `33,952/s` (`p50/p99 = 511.95 / 2,107.17 ms`) -> **`70,033/s` (`p50/p99 = 0.08 / 0.55 ms` — 6,400× lower p50)**.
- **Atomic `1:N` fan-out & real `ext4` durability gains**:
  - `fanout-1x4-q1`: `volatile` `125,392 -> 159,972 deliv/s` (+27.6 %, matching Mosquitto `159,998/s`); `log` `90,452 -> 138,762 deliv/s` (+53.4 %, `p50` `2.83 -> 0.84 ms`).
  - `fanout-1x16-q1`: `volatile` `127,912 -> 177,912 deliv/s` (+39.1 %); `log` `88,520 -> 155,216 deliv/s` (+75.3 %, `p50/p99` `6.13/13.16 -> 1.55/3.90 ms`).
  - `fanin-32x1-q1` (`log`): `3,297 -> 76,085 deliv/s` (**23.1×**); clean sub-millisecond steady state up to `60,000 deliv/s` (`0` refusals, `p50 = 0.03 ms` `vol` / `0.07 ms` `log`).
  - Real `ext4` `32p QoS 1 @ 60k/s` (`log-ext4`): `26,483 -> 40,855 deliv/s` (+54.3 %), `p50/p99 = 397.70 / 746.67 ms -> 4.11 / 22.22 ms` (**96.8× lower p50**).
- **Idle memory further reduced**: Plain TCP 768 connections drops from `5,269 B/conn` (Lot 8) to **`2,965 B/conn`** (`9,584 KiB` total vs Mosquitto `15,504 KiB`), and TLS 1.3 mTLS 400 connections drops from `14,746 B/conn` to **`12,616 B/conn`** (`13,488 KiB` total vs Mosquitto `31,344 KiB`).
- **Safety, purity, and verification**: `#![forbid(unsafe_code)]`, `#![no_std]` + `alloc` in `mqttiumd-contract`/`mqttiumd-core`, `boundary` (`17/17`), `BODY.sha256`, `1,591/1,591` default tests, `334/334` `kill-points`, `296/296` `test-hooks`, `274/274` oracle tests, `3/3` oracle campaigns, `64/64` compat unit tests, `23/23` mutants, `13/13` TLA+ models, and `0` open conformance items all pass.

## Not chosen

- **Resident multi-core `Delivery Owner` session sharding (`LogicalOwnerId(1..=K)`) in Lot 9**: ARM64 `perf` profiling (report [0040](../reports/0040-lot9-arm64-fairness-fanout-and-sharding.md)) confirms that after `MultiClaimedPublication`, the single Owner thread accounts for `91.8 %` (`fanout-1x16-vol`), `68.0 %` (`fanout-1x16-log`), `80.1 %` (`128p-q1-vol`), and `65.3 %` (`128p-q1-log`) of broker CPU, while `log-writer` sits at `22–24 %` (`~35 %` of one core) and both I/O threads sit at `4–10 %`. Splitting `Owner` into one `AdmissionOwner` plus `K` resident `DeliveryOwner` shards has an empirically measured budget of `~4.5 µs/delivery` of recipient-side CPU against `< 0.1 µs/delivery` for batched cross-core handoff (2–2.5× projected ceiling on 4-core ARM64 `log` before single-WAL saturation, and linear `K`-core scaling on `volatile` or multi-WAL servers). Implementing it cleanly requires splitting `Owner` state, adding multi-owner recovery tests in `mqttiumd-lab`, and updating TLA+ models (`models/`). Lot 9 delivers the prerequisites (`Connections`/`Deliveries` encapsulation, atomic `MultiClaimedPublication`, and empirical ARM64 `perf` budgeting) and defers the multi-owner runtime split to a dedicated Lot 10.
- **Pushing `SessionAction::Open { clean_start: false }` behind `inflight_route_ops`**: Explicitly rejected; resume/takeover connections do not mutate routes and must preserve reserved-lane progress even behind a saturated ordinary write lane.
